Card Design

Legends of Runeterra

My custom cards for Legends of Runeterra on display. I highlight a single champion who has a strong thematic design based on the champion's lore with the gameplay bringing the lore to life. Then, a small collection of cards are compiled below for perusal.

Featured Cards

Four cards with a closer look at the design philosophy behind them.

Shaco - Level 1

Shaco is a trickster known as an assassin who plays tricks on his victims, sometimes making them wonder if he was even real. He capitalizes on weakness, exemplified in his ability to harm those already hurt.

Shaco - Level 2

After Shaco levels up, he thrives on dancing around the enemy and their defenses, acting as a constant threat to the opponent's attempts at fending Shaco off.

Shaco's Origin

Shaco's Origin focuses on the fleeting nature of his presence, always popping in and out after playing his tricks. He also turns his own weakness into an onslaught. That, along with playing on the theme of fear, Shaco is a relentless storm of fending off fleeting nightmares.

Shaco's Signature Spell

The signature spell should highlight, work in tandem with, and exacerbate Shaco's game plan. Being able to remove ephemeral entirely while bouncing out of danger is a strong move that can shift the momentum of a battlefield.
