About

I'm Connor Hopkins. This is my portfolio for Game Design.

For over 8 years, I have been obsessed with developing for games that I fell in love with. This includes Daggerheart, League of Legends, and Legends of Runeterra. I made classes, subclasses, domains, and a separate system for Daggerheart. I made champions for League of Legends and a variety of cards for Legends of Runeterra. These can total to over hundreds of hours in dedicated time studying game design, exploring ability and power fantasies, and ensuring cohesion across individual mechanics to tell a singular story. Thanks for exploring some of my work! Feel free to reach out any time.
