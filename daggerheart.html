Daggerheart Homebrew

Daggerheart:
Where memories are built on laughter, sorrow, and epic moments.

Here, you will find a few examples of my Daggerheart designs, varying in scope. The goal of these designs are to provide tools for players to be thrilled in seeing a fantasy come to life, all while having a blast doing it! With a class, subclass, domain, and a whole new system, I hope you find a moment of thrill while looking around.

Adversary

Colossus

There's no hope in backing down because it can cross fields in a single step, so fight.

ValorReign, the deity of preservation, justice, and protection. In one hand, they wield a two-handed claymore. In the other, a bulwark shield. What hope does a hero have against this moving mountain? It's exposed crystal heart, always spewing out corruption to remain pure. Their T-shape visor in the helmet and their fullplate armor capable of bearing the weight of making judgements are a sign of strength to those zealous enough to any pure, non-combative cause. To those inclined to harm or fickleness, ValorReign is a force of destruction.

Tier 4 Colossus

Avatar of ValorReign, the Justiciar

A resplendent visage of mountain and sky, ice and fire, bulwark and claymore.

Motives & Tactics: Preserve, Protect, Vindicate

Size

350 ft. tall, 200 ft. wide

Segments

1 Head, 1 Chest, 1 Bulwark Arm, 1 Claymore Arm, 2 Legs

Thresholds 35 / 75
Stress 6
Experience Huge +4, Pass Judgement +3, Purge +3
Action Pass Judgement

When a PC rolls with Fear, all PCs in Very Far range lose a Hope.

Reaction Colossal Power

When ValorReign fails an attack, you gain a Fear.

ValorReign Head

Adjacent Segments: Chest

Difficulty 18
HP 6
Passive Cascade

If this segment is Destroyed, the Chest must mark 2 HP.

Passive Tandem Fatality

If this segment is Destroyed and the Chest is Destroyed, ValorReign is defeated.

Action Voice of Judgement

Mark a Stress to channel ValorReign's true voice through their avatar. PCs within Close range must succeed on a Knowledge Reaction roll or become temporarily Doomed. While Doomed, replace the Hope die with a d8 and the Fear die with a d20. Remove Doomed by having all of their Hope slots filled or when they clear a HP.

ValorReign Chest

Adjacent Segments: Bulwark Arm, Claymore Arm, Legs

Difficulty 21
HP 6
Passive Cascade

If this segment is Destroyed, the Head must mark 2 HP.

Passive Tandem Fatality

If this segment is Destroyed and the Head is Destroyed, ValorReign is defeated.

Passive Fragile

When a PC causes ValorReign to mark 2 or more HP, mark an additional HP.

Passive Strike (Very Close)

The Chest is immune to damage from attacks not made within Very Close range.

Action Behold Purification

Mark a Stress to force all PCs within sight to make a Presence Reaction roll as they behold the expulsion of corruption from ValorReign's heart. On a failure, they take 2d10+2 direct magic damage and recall a moment where they were torn between two choices, gaining a token. If the PC already has a token, they must lose all Hope and can't gain any Hope until ValorReign is Destroyed.

Reaction Exposure

When a PC targets the chest while on it, they must mark a Stress.

Reaction Explosive Purity

When the Chest marks it's last HP, all PCs within sight make the same Presence Reaction roll from Behold Purification.

ValorReign Bulwark Arm

Adjacent Segments: Chest

Difficulty 20
HP 6
ATK +4
Bulwark — Very Close, 2d8+8 phy
Passive Armored

When the Bulwark marks HP from an attack, it marks 1 fewer HP.

Passive Strike (Melee)

The Bulwark is immune to damage from attacks not made within Melee range.

Action Entomb

Mark a Stress to force all PCs on a single segment or within Very Close range of a target to make an Agility Reaction roll. On a failure, they are temporarily Restrained and must mark 1d4 Armor Slots without gaining the benefits.

Reaction Vault

When the Bulwark succeeds against a PC, that PC gains a token. If the PC already has a token, remove the token and they must vault a Domain card. They can pay the Recall cost to return it to their Loadout as normal.

Reaction Unlock The Vault

When a PC causes the Bulwark to mark 2 or more HP, they can recall a domain card from their vault without paying the Recall cost.

ValorReign Claymore Arm

Adjacent Segments: Chest

Difficulty 18
HP 6
ATK +5
Claymore — Close, 3d12+12 phy
Passive Blade's Edge

While on the Claymore, a PC is Vulnerable.

Action Deific Ferocity

On a successful attack, spend a Fear to deal 3d20+9 physical damage instead.

ValorReign Legs (2)

Adjacent Segments: Chest

Difficulty 15
HP 5 (each)
Passive Climbing (-2)

An action roll to climb on the leg gains a -2 penalty to its Difficulty.

Action Stride

Spend a Fear to move up to Far range. All creatures in that path must make an Agility Reaction Roll. Targets who fail take 2d12+8 physical damage and must lose a Hope. For each target who succeeds, you gain a Fear. ValorReign can't use this action if they are Collapsed.

Reaction Full Collapse

Once ValorReign's Legs are Destroyed, ValorReign Collapses and PCs can climb on the Chest and Arms from the ground.

Sanctums

A place an adventurer can call home.

"Sanctums" are a completely new system within the Daggerheart play space. Should a player decide that they want to dive deeper into a given fantasy, the Sanctums system will allow for a relatively low Cognitive Load increase, while providing more exciting focuses on fantasy and mechanical impacts to their character.

Overview

Sanctums and Facilities

Each domain emulates a specific theme, scaling in mechanical power based on levels. Sanctums are domain-driven in what power a player can tap in to, while Facilities are generic additives, allowing for a player to furhter customize where their power comes from and what shape it takes.


Of course, this boost of power can come at a definitive cost...
