Daggerheart Homebrew

Daggerheart:
Where memories are built on laughter, sorrow, and epic moments.

Here, you will find a few examples of my Daggerheart designs, varying in scope. The goal of these designs are to provide tools for players to be thrilled in seeing a fantasy come to life, all while having a blast doing it! With a class, subclass, domain, and a whole new system, I hope you find a moment of thrill while looking around.

Class

Summoner

A friend is never far away... a subordinate would also do.

The Summoner is focused on controlling a space either by bolstering allies or subjugating adversaries. Should they so choose, they can rely solely on themselves, summoning their own creatures and subordinates. When the Summoner enters a room, they are never alone and any who oppose them could very well find themselves a subject under the Summoner's thumb.

Domain: Domination Domain: Grace
Why this fits Daggerheart Daggerheart has focused primarily on a hero being the source of power, with a few explorations of other entities being integrated via constructs and the Beastbound Ranger. Devoting a whole class to a Summoner was a deliberate decision, as this fantasy can be expanded past the scope of a subclass. Potential subclasses would include:
  • Swarm
  • Behemoth
  • Totem
  • Sacrificer

Class Features

Summon

After a short rest, place a number of tokens equal to your Spellcast trait. Spend any number of tokens to summon a Summons within Close range for each token.
You can command Summons to perform simple, non-hostile actions.
During combat, you can spotlight 1 Summons. For each Summons after, you must mark a Stress. When you take Severe damage, remove one Summon. Otherwise, remove any existing Summons at the end of the scene.
A Summon has an attack range of Melee and uses a d6 for damage rolls using your Proficiency. At each Tier, their damage die increases by one die size. (d6 becomes d8, d8 becomes d10, etc...) They can move up to Close range when you spotlight them.

Array of Skills

During your spotlight, you can mark a Stress to do one of the following:

  • Spotlight an amount of Summons equal to your tier.
  • On a successful attack by a Summons, the target is temporarily Vulnerable.

Strength In Numbers

For each summons, gain a +1 to your Severe Threshold.

Hope Feature

Beckon

Spend 3 Hope to gain 1d4 summons tokens, up to your Spellcast trait.

Subclass

Sacrificer — Summoner

For when others are just pawns in your game.

Instead of focusing on sustaining your summoned creatures, they turn into batteries. When a Sacrificer needs to tap into their overwhelming strength, those that are under their rule will serve their finite, honorable purpose.

Spellcast trait: Presence.

Designer Note Giving a PC the chance to narrate something so awesome in scale but potentially morally complex leads to compelling table dynamics. There is also room within the subclass ecosystem for a more selfish playstyle that doesn't actively detract from other PCs, but rather is a unique resource that the Summoner PC manages.

Foundation

Bolster Me

When you succeed on an attack roll or spellcast roll that deals damage and there is a summon within Very Close range, you gain bonus damage equal to your tier to the damage roll for each summons.

Consume The Weak

When you would mark your last HP and there is a summons within Very Close range, you can instead remove the summons and clear a HP instead.

Specialization

Always Loyal

A summons can move up to Far range to get within Melee range of you. If this is used when an attack roll succeeds against you, you can remove the summons to add disadvantage to the attack roll.

The Gift of Servants

When you use a downtime move, you can use any unspent summons tokens to perform an addtional downtime move.

Mastery

Repurposed Servant

You can remove any number of your summoned creatures within Melee range to add a +8 to your attack roll or +12 to your damage roll each. Gain Hope or clear HP equal to how much your target marks. If this causes the target to mark their last HP, gain 1 summons token.

Subclass

Behemoth — Summoner

Grow until nothing can stop you.

Take the strength of an army and condense it into a singular Summons, a demi-god under your control.

Spellcast trait: Presence

Design Note This subclass could negatively blur the lines with the Beastbound Ranger. To avoid that, we can lean on the design behind the Beastbound being focused around a parternship and how the mechanics represent that dependency on one another. For this subclass, it should be focused on the Summons being a tool, non-autonomous, and separate the idea of a "partnership" with "master".

Foundation

The One

Your Summons damage die starts as a d8.

Combined Might

When you spend a summons token while a Summons exists, a new Summons doesn't appear. Instead, for each token spent, the existing Summons gains the following:

  • +4 on damage rolls
  • Grows in size
  • Increases your Severe threshold by +1

Specialization

Familiar Voice

Your Summons obeys your commands comfortably. When you make an action roll that involves your Summons, gain a +2.

Expanded Presence

When your Summons is existing while trying to persuade or intimdate someone, you can spend a Hope to add the following:

  • On a Hope, gain an additional Hope.
  • On a Fear, you can remove 2 unspent Summons tokens to stop the GM from gaining a Fear.

Mastery

Unbreakable Will

When you take Severe damage, instead of your Summons being removed, you can mark a Stress.

As One

Once per rest, when you spend a summons token that would create a Summons, you can spend a Hope to become a copy of it and must spend a Hope for every action after. If you can't, you drop out of the copied form. Your Summons can use your weapon feature on it's rolls. You and your Summons can move up to Far range without having to make an Agility Reaction roll. You can spend a hope to swap positions with your Summons within far range of each other.

Subclass

Swarm — Summoner

Endless. All-consuming. Don't fear the inevitable.

Open the portal to an endless wave of your will, drowning anyone who stands in the way.

Spellcast trait: Presence

Design Note When it comes to overwhelming masses, that can be hard to do with the Limit Cognitive Load design principle. The Swarm subclass is a player's opportunity to play a Leader type adversary, since those are the mechanics I borrowed to make it possible.

Foundation

The Many

Gain the Swarm Sheet. Your Summons damage die starts as a d4. Instead of gaining summons tokens up to your Spellcast trait, gain tokens equal to your level or Spellcast trait, whichever is higher.

Weaker Wills

Instead of marking Stress to command additional Summons, you can also choose to spend a Hope.

Greater Command

For each Summons you would spotlight, you can spotlight as many as equal to your tier.

Specialization

Unrelenting

When a Summons succeeds on an attack roll with Fear, you gain a Hope.

Collective Mark

When a Summons makes an attack roll and there are other Summons within Very Close range of the target, you can make a Spellcast roll (16). On a success, you can move the Summons to that target and add to the attack, dealing half damage.

Mastery

Volatile Mitosis

When you mark Severe damage, roll d6s equal to your Spellcast trait. On a 6, choose a Summons. It splits into two Volatile Summons. The next time they obey your command, they are removed.

Domain

Domination

The mastery of command, authority, and leadership.

Domination is the domain of authority and leadership. With the strength of a commander both in voice and in arm, those who practice their ability to cut through the noise of a battlefield have ultimate sway over who does what, when they want. Wielders of Domination are promised obedience and they, in turn, lead inspiring victories.

Why this fits Daggerheart Daggerheart hosts a variety of domains that include attributes like inspiration, leadership, and persuasion. However, none of them deliberately focus on what it would look like to have a true leader that can manipulate both friend and foe. This open space allows for a domain that focuses on players wanting a fantasy where their words matter, where they can influence the enemy past opposition, and make allies push past their usual limits.

Guiding Words

Ability

When a PC within Close range makes a successful attack, you can inspire them. Mark a Stress to give them a +1 Proficiency for the damage roll. When an adversary within Close range makes a successful attack, you can cause them to lose concentration on who their target is supposed to be. Mark a Stress to cause them to roll 1 fewer damage die.

Adversary

Colossus

There's no hope in backing down because it can cross fields in a single step, so fight.

ValorReign, the deity of preservation, justice, and protection. In one hand, they wield a two-handed claymore. In the other, a bulwark shield. What hope does a hero have against this moving mountain? It's exposed crystal heart, always spewing out corruption to remain pure. Their T-shape visor in the helmet and their fullplate armor capable of bearing the weight of making judgements are a sign of strength to those zealous enough to any pure, non-combative cause. To those inclined to harm or fickleness, ValorReign is a force of destruction.

System

Sanctums

A place an adventurer can call home.

"Sanctums" are a completely new system within the Daggerheart play space. Should a player decide that they want to dive deeper into a given fantasy, the Sanctums system will allow for a relatively low Cognitive Load increase, while providing more exciting focuses on fantasy and mechanical impacts to their character.

Overview

Sanctums and Facilities

Each domain emulates a specific theme, scaling in mechanical power based on levels. Sanctums are domain-driven in what power a player can tap in to, while Facilities are generic additives, allowing for a player to furhter customize where their power comes from and what shape it takes.


Of course, this boost of power can come at a definitive cost...
