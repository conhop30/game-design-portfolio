Game Design Portfolio

Building tools for hopeful fantasies to become heroic realities.

I'm Connor Hopkins, a guy who can't let go of the idea that everyone deserves a space to feel empowered in every facet; emotionally, creatively, recognized, and personally sacrificed for. Game design isn't just about having fun; it's about connecting with themself and those around them as human beings. We're all in this together, and games are a safe place to explore our hopes, desires, and ambitions to be that hero we can all be outside of the game. Welcome to a space of intentional game design born of love for games and a gentle caring for those who participate.
